## 1. HOW THE COLLEGE RUNS — two rule layers
- MAKAUT LAYER: degree, curriculum, exam conduct, registration, affiliation, exam fees.
  The college cannot override these; it enforces them on the university's behalf.
- COLLEGE LAYER: fees, hostel, admission quota, scholarships, services, academic regs.
  The college sets these autonomously (autonomous status from 2024-25) and CAN change them.

Autonomy legal basis (verified): UGC letter F.2-10/2023/(AC-Policy) + MAKAUT letter
7.30/MAKAUT/Autonomy(BCREC)/2024/567, autonomy conferred April 2024.
Autonomy scope verbatim (BCREC Academic Regulations §3): institute independently develops
schemes of instruction, syllabi, examination methods; obtains Academic Council + Governing
Body approval; publishes its own results/grade cards/transcripts/PDC — EXCEPT the degree
certificate (MAKAUT-only). Curriculum still "adhering to University's minimum standards".
